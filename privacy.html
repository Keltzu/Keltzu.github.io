Cosmo Cascade

Privacy Policy · Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Cosmo Cascade (“the Game”) handles information. The Game is developed by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). We want to keep things simple and transparent.

1. Summary

  • The Game does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your scores and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device information to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, we ask for your consent before ads use your data for personalisation.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your best score and your settings (sound volume and vibration on/off) locally on your device. This information is never sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited, to display ads (for example, the optional “Continue” ad and ads between games). To provide this service, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (for example, the Android Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (such as device model, operating system and app version),
  • information about your interaction with ads (for example, ads shown or tapped).

This information is used by Google to deliver, personalise (where permitted) and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you personally.

Learn more about how Google uses information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4. Your consent and choices

If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the Game shows a consent message (provided through Google’s User Messaging Platform) the first time you open it. You can choose whether ads may use your data for personalisation. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy settings inside the Game.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device’s settings (on Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; the exact path may vary by device).

5. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help to address it.

6. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR applies, personalised advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time as described above. Non-personalised advertising and fraud prevention are based on legitimate interests in operating a free, ad-supported game.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (in Finland: the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman, tietosuoja.fi). Because we do not store personal data ourselves, requests regarding advertising data are best directed to Google, but you are welcome to contact us and we will assist you.

8. Security

The Game only connects to the internet to load ads and consent forms, using encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when the Game gets new features. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact
